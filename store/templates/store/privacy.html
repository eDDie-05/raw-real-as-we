Information collected

When you place an order, RAW stores the details needed to review and fulfil it: your name, phone number, optional email, delivery location, selected products, and order history. An account stores the profile and addresses you choose to save. Newsletter signup stores your email address.

Why we use it

Order and contact details are used to confirm orders, arrange delivery, respond to requests and maintain account history. Newsletter addresses are used for RAW updates until you unsubscribe.

WhatsApp

When you choose to open WhatsApp, your device opens a pre-filled order message addressed to RAW at +255 775 794 221. The website records that the WhatsApp link was opened. It cannot tell whether you sent the message.

Data safety & control

Access to customer records is restricted to authorized store staff. Contact RAW to correct your information or request account assistance. This site does not process payment-card information.

Cookies

The store uses the session cookie required to maintain your shopping bag and account session. It does not use advertising trackers in this starter build.
